# staysvic: City or Coast? Short-Stay Demand in Melbourne and Regional Victoria

Helps someone thinking about running a short-stay rental compare the
Melbourne CBD with the Great Ocean Road, the Mornington Peninsula,
Geelong and other Victorian towns. Bundles cleaned Inside Airbnb
listings and reviews and Homes Victoria median rents, with functions
that measure seasonal demand, who operates the listings, and how many
booked nights a short stay needs to out-earn a normal lease. Includes a
Shiny app for exploring these questions.
